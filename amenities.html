Amenities

Everything within a morning walk.

Parks, a badminton court, two gyms, a temple, a Mother Dairy and parking — the everyday is already inside the boundary. The market, schools and hospitals are just outside the gates.

Central park Badminton court Temple Basement parking

Inside the boundary

The society, in daily use.

These are the facilities residents use without leaving the society — a short walk from every block.

Central Park

The society's main green — walks, cricket and evening gatherings.

Kids' park

Play equipment and open ground, kept away from the internal traffic.

Badminton court

Outdoor court, busy through the early morning and evening.

Morning walk track

A loop through the grounds, shaded for most of the morning.

Indoor gym

Weights and equipment for residents, with a physiotherapy centre alongside.

Open gym

Outdoor training stations in the park, open through the day.

Physiotherapy centre

On-site treatment room, convenient for older residents.

The temple

At the centre of the complex, beside the Mother Dairy and the park.

Mother Dairy

Milk, curd and daily essentials without leaving the society.

SBI ATM

Cash point inside the boundary, near the daily services.

Post and library vans

India Post and Delhi Public Library mobile services visit the society.

Basement and open parking

Covered bays under the multi-storey blocks, open parking along the boundary.

Outside the gates

The market is next door.

Gate 1 opens opposite the market and New Ashok Nagar colony, so most everyday services are a short walk from the boundary.

Daily essentials

Newspaper vendor, tea stall, coffee shop, milkman, bread and eggs, fast food, ice cream, paan shop and tiffin services.

Home and help

Cooks, maids, sweepers, drivers, car cleaners, dry cleaning and ironing, electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, pest control, furniture and AC rental.

Health

General physicians and specialists, a child specialist, a gynaecologist, a 24-hour chemist, nursing homes and hospitals.

Schools and services

Schools and colleges, day care, cable and broadband, mobile and laptop repair, salons and beauty parlours, property dealers, home loans, insurance and tax consultants.

Facility lists are drawn from the society's published information and the surrounding New Ashok Nagar market. Availability of individual services may change.
