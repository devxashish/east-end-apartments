05 · Society Heritage

The scale of a town, the calm of a lane.

East End Apartments is one of East Delhi's largest co-operative housing societies: 958 flats in 29 blocks of eight and nine floors, and 348 low-rise C-Type homes arranged around lawns, a temple and a central park.

Four gates keep the rhythm simple. Residents come in from Gate 1, visitors through Gate 2 opposite the metro, and services at Gate 3 — so the lanes inside stay quiet.
